---
title: "Who is Solomon for?"
description: "Solomon is designed for a specific kind of person. You may well be one of them."
---

Solomon is designed for adults who want to change their relationship with alcohol - but who don't feel at home in traditional recovery settings.

That covers a lot of ground. It might mean you're drinking more than you'd like and want to understand why. It might mean you've tried to cut down before and found it harder than expected. It might mean you're doing fine by most measures but privately know something isn't right.

You don't need a diagnosis. You don't need to have hit a particular kind of bottom. You just need to want something to be different.

---

## The people Solomon is built for

Solomon tends to resonate most with people who:

- Are high-functioning and don't identify with the label of "addict" or "alcoholic"
- Find the idea of group settings or traditional recovery culture a barrier rather than a gateway
- Have tried willpower-based approaches and found them unsustainable
- Are curious about sobriety but not yet sure they're ready
- Are privately struggling in a way they haven't been able to talk about
- Are already in recovery and want a daily companion alongside other support
- Want structure and support without it feeling clinical or managed

None of these are requirements. They're just the kinds of people who tend to find Sol genuinely useful.

---

## If you're not sure you have a problem

That's fine. Solomon is particularly well-suited to this moment - before the label, before the decision, when you're just starting to ask questions.

Sol's opening is simply: *"What's brought you here today?"* He will meet you wherever you are. Curiosity about your relationship with alcohol is enough of a reason to start a conversation.

---

## If you're already in treatment

Solomon can sit comfortably alongside professional treatment - as a daily companion, a space to process, or a way to stay connected to your reasons for change between sessions.

If you're in a structured programme, Sol is not a replacement for it. He's a supplement - available whenever you need him, with no waiting room.

---

## Who Solomon is not designed for

<Warning>
  Solomon is not appropriate as a standalone tool for people with severe physical dependency on alcohol. If you drink heavily or daily, and experience physical symptoms when you don't drink - shaking, sweating, anxiety - please speak to your GP before making any changes. Medically supervised support may be necessary. See the [Medical and Safety](/medical/physical-dependency-and-withdrawal) section for full guidance.
</Warning>

Solomon is also not designed for:

- People under 18
- Use by a professional on behalf of a client
- Use as a proxy for someone else's recovery without their knowledge
