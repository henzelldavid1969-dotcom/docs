---
title: "Forgotten your password"
description: "How to reset your password and get back into your account."
---

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it in a couple of steps. Your account, memory, and conversation history are all safe - a password reset doesn't affect any of them.

---

## How to reset your password

1. On the Solomon sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Tap **Send reset link**
4. Check your email - a reset link will arrive within a few minutes
5. Tap the link in the email and follow the steps to set a new password
6. Sign in with your new password

---

## If the email doesn't arrive

Check your spam or junk folder first - reset emails occasionally end up there.

If it's still not there after a few minutes:

- Make sure you've entered the email address you used when you created your account
- Try requesting the reset link again
- If the problem persists, contact support and we'll help you get back in

[Contact support →](/support/contact-us)

---

## If you're not sure which email you used

Try the addresses you use most commonly. If you're still stuck, contact support with any email addresses you might have used and we'll look into it.

---

## If you never set a password

It's possible to have a Solomon account without a password - this happens if you saved your conversation when Sol offered, but skipped the optional password step.

In this case, use the forgotten password flow above to create one. Enter the email you provided when you saved your chat, and a reset link will be sent to you.
