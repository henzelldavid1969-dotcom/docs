---
title: "Using Solomon on mobile"
description: "Solomon is designed for your phone. Here's how to get the best from it."
---

Solomon is built mobile-first. It is designed to be used on your phone - in the moments when you need it, wherever you are.

It runs in the browser. There is nothing to download from an app store.

---

## iPhone

Solomon is optimised for Safari on iPhone. Open Safari, go to your Solomon link, and it will work as you'd expect.

**For the best experience, add Solomon to your home screen.**

This makes Solomon open like a native app - full screen, no browser chrome, with its own icon on your home screen.

To do this:
1. Open Solomon in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Give it a name and tap **Add**

Solomon will now appear on your home screen and open directly into the app.

<Note>
  Solomon uses Safari's built-in security for authentication. If you use a different browser on iPhone - Chrome, Firefox, or others - some features may not work correctly. Safari is strongly recommended.
</Note>

---

## Android

On Android, Solomon works in Chrome. Open Chrome, navigate to your Solomon link, and it will run without issue.

To add to your home screen on Android:
1. Open Solomon in Chrome
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top right
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**
4. Confirm

---

## A note on keyboard behaviour

On mobile, the keyboard appears when you tap the message input. Solomon is designed to handle this gracefully - the conversation scrolls to keep Sol's last message and your input visible.

If you find the layout behaving unexpectedly, try rotating your phone to landscape and back, or closing and reopening the app from your home screen.

---

## Notifications

Solomon does not currently send push notifications. If you want to keep a regular check-in habit, setting a personal reminder on your phone is the simplest approach.
