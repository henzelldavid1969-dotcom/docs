title: "Signing in"
description: "How to access your account on any device."
---

If you have a Solomon account, you can sign in from any device and Sol will know you - your memory, your history, and your name will all be there.

---

## How to sign in

When you open Solomon, you'll see a sign-in screen if you're not already logged in. Enter the email address and password you used when you created your account and tap **Sign in**.

Sol will greet you by name and pick up from where you last left off.

---

## Staying signed in

Solomon keeps you signed in on your device until you explicitly sign out or your session expires. If you've added Solomon to your home screen, you'll typically open straight into your conversation without needing to sign in each time.

Sessions do expire periodically for security reasons. If you find yourself on the sign-in screen unexpectedly, this is why - just sign in again. Your conversations and memory are unaffected.

---

## Signing in on a new device

Your account is not tied to a specific device. Go to your Solomon link on any phone or browser, enter your email and password, and everything will be there.

---

## If you set up your account without a password

If you saved your chat when Sol offered but skipped the password step, you won't have a password set yet. You can request a password reset to create one.

[Forgotten your password? →](/getting-started/forgotten-password)

---

## Signing out

To sign out, go to **Settings** and tap **Sign out**. Your account, memory, and history are all preserved - signing out simply ends your session on that device.
