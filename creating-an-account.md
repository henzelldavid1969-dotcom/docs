You don't need an account to use Solomon. You can open the app and start talking to Sol immediately - no sign-up, no email, nothing required.

An account becomes worth having once you've had a conversation worth keeping.

---

## How it works

After around six messages, Sol will offer to save your conversation. This is a natural moment in the flow - not a pop-up or an interruption. Sol explains what he's doing and why, and you can decline without anything changing.

If you choose to save, you provide:

- Your name (any name is fine - it doesn't need to be your real one)
- Your email address

That's it. At this point a free account is created and Sol begins building a memory of you.

A short time later, Sol will gently offer to set a password. This is optional - you can skip it and continue without one. Setting a password means you can sign in from any device and your history will be there.

## Anonymous use

If you choose not to create an account, you can continue using Solomon anonymously for a limited number of messages per session. Anonymous sessions don't carry memory - each time you open the app, Sol starts fresh with no knowledge of previous conversations.

This is a reasonable way to use Solomon if you want to try it without committing to anything. If you later decide you want memory and history, you can create an account at any point.

---

## Your data

Creating an account means Solomon stores your name, email address, and a memory summary of your conversations. No one at Solomon reads your chats. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties.
