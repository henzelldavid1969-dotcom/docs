## Important to know

<Warning>
  Solomon is not a medical product and is not a substitute for professional treatment. If you are physically dependent on alcohol, please speak to your GP before making any changes. See the [Medical and Safety](/medical/not-a-medical-product) section for more.
</Warning>

## If you need help right now

Solomon is a companion, not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, please reach out to one of the following:

| Service | Contact |
|---|---|
| Samaritans | 116 123 - free, 24 hours |
| Drinkline | 0300 123 1110 |
| NHS 111 | For urgent physical health concerns |
| Emergency services | 999 |
